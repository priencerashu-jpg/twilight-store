After you send the order through WhatsApp, our team will review the details and confirm availability and the next steps.

Contact us with your ordering phone number, clear photos or video and a short description of the issue. Our team will review the request and explain the available resolution.

How can I contact customer support?

Use either WhatsApp number shown on the Contact page. For faster help, include the product name, preferred option, quantity and delivery district.
