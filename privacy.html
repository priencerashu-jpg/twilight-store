Cart information

Your product choices, quantities and delivery-area preference are stored in your browser so the cart can remain available without an account. Clearing browser data removes this saved cart, and it does not automatically sync to another browser or device.

Information you choose to send

Name, phone number, address, order number and message details are used only when you enter them and choose to continue through WhatsApp. The website does not save those contact-form entries in an online customer account.

Analytics and cookies

TikTok Pixel is loaded only after you choose “Allow analytics” in our privacy notice. If allowed, TikTok may receive technical and usage information under its own privacy terms. You can change your preference from the footer.

A privacy-focused page counter is loaded on individual product pages to display total website visits. It is not used to create a Twilight Market customer account.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google Fonts, TikTok and the product-page counter operate under their own policies when their services are opened or loaded. We recommend reviewing their privacy information.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to handle order conversations responsibly. WhatsApp conversations and order records may be retained for customer support, fulfilment and legitimate business records.

Your choices

You may continue without analytics, clear your browser cart, update cookie preferences or contact us to ask about information shared directly with Twilight Market.

Need clarification?

Message the Twilight Market team before ordering and we will explain the relevant details.
