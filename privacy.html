Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the information used by this website.

01

Browser cart storage

Your cart and preferred delivery area are saved in your browser using local storage so the same device can remember your selections. This information is not an account and does not sync between devices.

02

Order and contact details

Name, phone number, address and contact-form details are not saved by this website. They are placed into a WhatsApp message only after you choose to send it. WhatsApp then handles that message under its own privacy terms.

03

TikTok Pixel

The website includes TikTok Pixel to help understand visits and marketing performance. TikTok may use cookies or similar technologies and receive device, browser and interaction information according to TikTok's own privacy practices.

04

Visit counter

Individual product pages display a page-visit counter provided by Hits. It measures page requests and is not presented as a count of people currently online.

05

Your choices

You can clear the saved cart and browser data at any time through your browser. You can also use browser privacy controls to limit cookies or tracking technologies.
